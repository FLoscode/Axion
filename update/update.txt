Key Fixes & Architecture Integration
1. Server Crash Fix & Unified Backend: 

app.py
Root Cause of Previous Crash: On Windows with Python 3.13, printing Unicode box-drawing characters without UTF-8 console output reconfigured raised a fatal UnicodeEncodeError: 'charmap' codec can't encode characters.
Fix: Added automatic UTF-8 stream reconfiguration (sys.stdout.reconfigure(encoding='utf-8', errors='replace')), safe ASCII console banners, and non-blocking terminal guards.
Logistics Simulator Background Engine: Integrated 

logistics_sim.py
 into a continuous background daemon thread running in app.py. Drives live AIS vessel tracking (RRS Sir David Attenborough), field expedition Iridium pings, fuel farm depletion, and simulated anomalies (VESSEL_OFF_COURSE, FIELD_TEAM_SOS).
